We want to undestand when there a dead ball situations (D flag) - stop of play
A dead ball interval has a begining and an end.

For now we fix (assume) the following:
    - The end of a D flag is determined by the smart-tagging data 

-> Which smart tags resemble D-flags?
    Tags with a 'Type': Throw-in, Corner Kick, Goal Kick, Free Kick, Kick Off (After Goal), Kick Off (Start)

    They have 'timestamp' and 'end_timestamp', we'll take 'end_timestamp' or the average of the two (in between them), if the smart tag ends too far after the actual event.

    This way we fix the end of the D flag interval.

-> How to determine the start of the D flag interval?
    Either:
     1. we get some extra info for the beginning of the interval, or 
     2. we get the leght of the D flag inteval and append it before the end.

    As a first test, we want to infer the D-flag intervals from the broadcast replay lengths of the nearest replay before the smart tag, which tells us the end point of the D-flag interval. 

    For this we need to synchronise the broadcast to the tactical video.
    Then we need to get replays from the broadcast somehow - they have 

    Why this is a BAD IDEA - there aren't transitions for the end of the replay. I.e. no clear or consistant visual cues for the replays straight from the video.

    We won't use replays!
    We will use missing homography + missing ball intervals from the broadcast - also maybe add missing ball intervals from the tactical vid as well.

-> How do we determine more precisely the end of the D-flag interval?
    Use ball velocity and acceleration: We want to flag the moment when the ball gets a big acceleration (i.e. it is kicked by a player, or thrown).
    IMPORTANT: Only do this after we know where the D-flag interval roughly is! This is to replace the assumption of fixing the end of the interval to the endtimestamp of the smart tag.
